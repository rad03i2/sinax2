### SINAX Integration Notes
- Icons are curated, organized into semantic categories, standardized to 24x24 viewBox vector curves, and tinted dynamically via `currentColor` / SVG XML injection.
- Directional icons (e.g. back, forward, chevrons, undo, redo) are mirrored dynamically during runtime rendering based on application layout direction (RTL / LTR).\n
